Rubi Privacy Policy

In short: Rubi has no servers of its own. Everything you create in Rubi stays in your browser, or in your own Google account if you choose to connect it. Rubi has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never sells or shares your data, and it never uses your data for advertising.

1. Who we are

Rubi is a free, open-source new-tab extension for Google Chrome made by Mohammad Mashreghi. The source code is public at github.com/M-Mashreghi/Rubi. Contact: amiramirmashgh@gmail.com.

2. Data you create in Rubi

Your tasks, notes, shortcuts and folders, calendar events and birthdays, reminders, settings, wallpaper and other content are stored in your browser (Chrome extension storage, local storage and IndexedDB). If Chrome sync is on for extensions, Chrome may copy your Rubi settings and content to your Google account through Chrome's own sync service. The developer never receives this data.

3. Data from Google (Google sign-in is optional)

Rubi works fully without signing in. Two optional features use Google APIs, and only after you turn them on in Settings and approve Google's consent screen:

FeaturePermission (scope)What Rubi does with it
Save to Google Drivehttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdataSaves one backup file of your Rubi content in the hidden Rubi app folder of your own Google Drive and reads it back, so your content appears on your other computers. Rubi cannot see or open any of your other Drive files.
Show Google Calendar eventshttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonlyReads the list of your calendars and the events in the dates you are looking at, and shows them in Rubi's calendar. Rubi never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar. Events are kept only in memory while the page is open and are not saved or copied anywhere.
BothYour Google account email (Chrome identity)Shown in Settings so you know which account is connected.

Data from Google APIs goes directly between your browser and Google. It is never sent to the developer or to any other party.

Limited Use. Rubi's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Rubi:

  • uses Google user data only to provide the features described above, which you can see in Rubi;
  • does not transfer Google user data to anyone, except as needed to provide these features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your consent;
  • does not use or transfer Google user data for advertising, retargeting or personalised ads;
  • does not sell Google user data, or use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending;
  • does not allow any person to read Google user data, unless you ask for help and give consent, it is needed for security or to comply with law.

Stopping access. Turn the feature off in Rubi's Settings, or remove Rubi's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the Drive backup, disconnect Rubi from Drive in Google Drive's settings (Manage apps → Rubi → Delete hidden app data).

5. Browser permissions

  • Bookmarks and most-visited sites (asked only when you choose "Import from browser"): read once to let you pick shortcuts. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • Site flag (off by default, needs your approval): reads the server address of the page you open and looks up its country in a database bundled inside Rubi. The address never leaves your computer and no browsing history is kept.
  • Screenshots, snippets, focus mode and the floating timer: act on a page only when you use them.
  • Notifications, alarms and idle state: for the reminders, timers and breaks you set.
  • Microphone and camera (asked by Chrome only when you use voice search, the voice changer or the live camera background): the sound and picture are processed inside the Rubi page on your computer. They are never recorded to a server, saved by Rubi or sent anywhere; the camera turns off when you leave the tab.
  • Photos for the coffee-cup reading are looked at inside the page only and are not saved or sent.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete items one by one, or remove the extension, which deletes everything Rubi stored in the browser. Data in your Google account (Drive backup, Chrome sync) can be removed as described above.

7. Children

Rubi is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Earlier versions are kept in the project's public history on GitHub.

  • گوگل درایو (اختیاری): فقط یک فایل پشتیبان در پوشه‌ی مخفی روبی در درایو خودتان ذخیره می‌شود. روبی فایل‌های دیگر درایو شما را نمی‌بیند.
  • تقویم گوگل (اختیاری، فقط خواندنی): رویدادها فقط نمایش داده می‌شوند. روبی هیچ‌وقت چیزی در تقویم شما تغییر نمی‌دهد و رویدادها را جایی ذخیره نمی‌کند.
  • استفاده‌ی روبی از اطلاعات دریافتی از APIهای گوگل تابع «سیاست داده‌های کاربر سرویس‌های API گوگل» و الزامات Limited Use است.
  • میکروفون و دوربین: فقط وقتی از جستجوی صوتی، تغییر صدا یا پس‌زمینه‌ی زنده‌ی دوربین استفاده کنید کروم اجازه می‌خواهد. صدا و تصویر فقط داخل صفحه‌ی روبی روی کامپیوتر خودتان پردازش می‌شوند و جایی فرستاده یا نگه داشته نمی‌شوند؛ دوربین با بیرون رفتن از تب خاموش می‌شود.
  • عکس فال قهوه فقط داخل همان صفحه بررسی می‌شود و ذخیره یا فرستاده نمی‌شود.
  • ابزارهایی مثل آب‌وهوا، نرخ ارز، ترجمه و فوتبال فقط وقتی از آن‌ها استفاده کنید و فقط با اطلاعات لازم به سرویس مربوط وصل می‌شوند.
