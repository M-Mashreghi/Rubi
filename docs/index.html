What Rubi does

Rubi replaces Chrome's new tab page with a calm, customisable dashboard:

  • Calendar with the Persian (Solar Hijri), Gregorian and Islamic calendars, holidays, birthdays and your own events with reminders.
  • Tasks and notes, a read-later list and boards.
  • Shortcuts and folders for your favourite sites, plus a large list of services.
  • Tools: weather, currency rates, timers, focus sounds, translator, world clock, water and eye-break reminders and more.
  • Wallpapers, themes (including a Glass theme), fonts and four languages: Persian, English, French and German.

How Rubi uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional. Rubi works fully without it. If you choose to connect your account in Rubi's Settings:

  • Google Drive (drive.appdata): Rubi saves one backup of your Rubi content in its own hidden app folder in your Drive, so it appears on your other computers. Rubi cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): Rubi shows your Google Calendar events next to its own calendar. It only reads events and never changes your calendar.

Your data goes only between your browser and Google. Rubi has no servers and never sends your data to the developer or anyone else. Read the full privacy policy.

روبی چیست؟

روبی یک افزونه‌ی رایگان کروم است که صفحه‌ی تب جدید را به تقویم شمسی، قمری و میلادی، کارها، یادداشت‌ها، میان‌برها و ابزارهای روزانه تبدیل می‌کند. بدون تبلیغ و بدون ردیابی.

اتصال به گوگل اختیاری است. با آن می‌توانید اطلاعات روبی را در پوشه‌ی مخفی روبی در گوگل درایو خودتان نگه دارید و رویدادهای تقویم گوگل را ببینید. روبی فقط رویدادها را می‌خواند و چیزی را تغییر نمی‌دهد.
