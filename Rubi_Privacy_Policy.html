Privacy Policy for Rubi

Extension: Rubi | Your All-in-One Smart Assistant  •  Version reviewed: 3.0.0  •  Last updated: November 21, 2026

Rubi is a Chrome browser extension that replaces the new tab page with a customizable dashboard featuring tasks, notes, bookmarks and shortcuts, calendars, reminders, and optional productivity tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information Rubi handles and when it interacts with external services.

1. Information stored on your device

Rubi uses browser storage (including Chrome extension storage and, for some features, local storage) to remember your dashboard settings, language and appearance, tasks, notes, shortcuts, reminders, and other information you choose to enter or configure. This data can remain in your browser between sessions. Removing the extension or clearing its storage may remove locally stored information.

2. Optional Google integrations

If you choose to connect a Google account, Rubi may use Chrome's Google identity authorization to obtain access tokens and show basic account information, including your email address. Google Drive integration is designed to access application-specific storage for optional data synchronization or backup. Google Calendar integration may retrieve calendar or event information needed to display your calendar within Rubi. These integrations are optional and may require you to grant additional permissions. Google account and service data are handled only to provide the feature you activate.

Rubi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, where applicable.

3. Website and browser information

With your permission, Rubi may read browser bookmarks or frequently visited websites so you can import them into your dashboard. User-initiated tools may interact with the current page, such as saving links, inserting saved text snippets, capturing screenshots, or applying focus and site-specific features. An optional site-country indicator may use website request information and a bundled local IP-to-country database to display a country flag. Browser permissions are requested to support these functions and are not intended for advertising-based tracking.

4. External services and data transfers

Some optional features make requests to third-party services. Depending on which feature you use, the data sent may include a city name or location coordinates for weather or air-quality information; the text selected for translation; symbols or queries for financial and sports information; or data needed for Google service integrations. These services can receive normal technical information associated with web requests, such as an IP address.

Rubi may connect to Google APIs, Open-Meteo, Frankfurter, exchange-rate and market-data providers, Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, TheSportsDB, Google Translate, GitHub APIs, and other service endpoints used by individual tools. Each external service may process the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

Music recognition: When you explicitly use the music-recognition tool, a short recorded audio sample may be sent to AudD (api.audd.io) for identification. Do not use this feature if you do not want the selected audio to be transmitted to that service.

5. Notifications and timers

Rubi may use browser alarms, notifications, and idle status to support user-configured task reminders, focus sessions, and wellness reminders. These functions use permissions provided by Chrome and associated preferences saved in your browser.

6. Purpose and limits of use

Rubi uses information to operate its requested dashboard and productivity features. The extension is not intended to sell personal data, use information for targeted advertising, or assess creditworthiness. Any transmission to an external provider should be limited to what is needed for the tool or integration you choose to use.

7. Data retention and control

Locally saved data remains in your browser until you remove or clear it through available browser or extension controls. If you activate Google Drive synchronization, synchronized application data may remain in your connected Google account until removed there. You can disable optional integrations, revoke permissions through your browser or Google account, and uninstall Rubi. External providers may have separate retention practices for information sent to them.

8. Security

Rubi uses browser-provided permissions and HTTPS endpoints for its supported online integrations. No software or online transfer can guarantee absolute security. You should grant only the optional permissions needed for features you intend to use.

9. Children's privacy

Rubi is a general-purpose productivity extension and is not specifically designed for children. It does not include features intended to knowingly collect children's personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Rubi's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.
